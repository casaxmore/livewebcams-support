Keep exploring.

A few answers about cameras, discovery, location and Pro.

Why is a camera unavailable?

Camera images come from their source operators. Maintenance, weather, connection problems or source changes can interrupt a feed. Check your connection, refresh the app and try another camera. If it persists, email us the camera name, country and source.

Are all cameras live video?

Many sources provide periodically refreshed still images; some offer time-lapse video. Check the source image time when it is available. A camera may show an earlier image, darkness or limited visibility. Refresh rates and image quality depend on the source.

How do Discover and daylight suggestions work?

Open Discover, choose an interest or tap Surprise me. The daylight filter estimates daylight from the camera’s coordinates and time. It does not guarantee sunshine, visibility or that the latest image was just captured.

How can I enlarge an image?

Open a camera, tap Full screen and use two fingers to zoom and move around the image. The detail available depends on the original source resolution.

Do I need to share my location?

Location is optional and is used for Around Me while you use the app. You can explore the map, search and categories without granting it. Live Webcams does not offer location sharing with other people. Automatic AdMob coarse-location collection is disabled; IP-based general location estimation may still occur.

I purchased Pro. How do I restore it?

Open Settings in Live Webcams and select Restore purchases using the same Apple Account as the purchase. Pro removes ads and permanently unlocks supported continents and Pro benefits. If access is not restored, tell us what happens without sending passwords or full payment details.

How does temporary access work?

Where offered, completing an optional rewarded ad unlocks the selected continent for one hour. This is temporary access, separate from the permanent Pro purchase. The app displays the remaining access time. If the ad is unavailable, try again later.

Can I suggest a camera or an improvement?

Of course. Send the official source link and tell us why the place interests you. We review availability and reuse conditions before considering a camera. Inclusion is not guaranteed. Feature ideas are welcome too.
