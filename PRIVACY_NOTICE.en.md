# Support Website Privacy Notice – Live Webcams

**Last updated: November 23, 2026**

## Scope

This notice covers the Live Webcams support website and emails sent to its support address. The iPhone and iPad app has separate privacy information.

## Who is responsible?

José Manuel Casabona, a self-employed individual operating under the commercial name **Casaxmore**, is responsible for handling support enquiries. Contact: [casaxmore@gmail.com](mailto:casaxmore@gmail.com).

## What information is handled and why?

If you email us, we receive your email address, any name you provide, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. A support request may also include your device model, operating-system version, app version, and a description or screenshot of a problem.

We use this information to answer your enquiry, investigate issues, handle requests about app access or purchases, and follow up when necessary. Please provide only what is needed and do not send passwords, full payment details, or unrelated sensitive information. If you do not provide a reply address or enough information about the issue, we may be unable to respond or resolve it.

## Legal grounds

- **App support:** processing necessary to provide support connected with your use or purchase of the app, or to take steps you request before a contract, under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- **General enquiries and suggestions:** our legitimate interest in answering messages addressed to us and maintaining the support service, under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. You may object on grounds relating to your situation.
- **Legal duties and claims:** where necessary, compliance with applicable legal duties or our legitimate interest in establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

Support correspondence is not used to subscribe you to marketing. We do not make automated decisions about you or create advertising profiles from support messages.

## Email, hosting and international processing

We use **Google Gmail** to receive and reply to support emails. Your own email provider also processes your outgoing message under its own terms. This website does not send an email automatically: the contact button opens your email application and you decide whether to send it.

The website is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. We do not operate a separate visitor-analytics service on this site.

Google and GitHub may process information in countries outside the European Economic Area. The applicable locations, legal entities and transfer safeguards depend on their services and terms. Details of the safeguards, including applicable adequacy frameworks or standard contractual clauses, can be consulted in the providers’ information linked below:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en) and [Google data-transfer frameworks](https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks?hl=en).
- [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [GitHub Pages information](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages).

We do not sell support correspondence. Access is limited to what is needed to handle the request, provide these services, or comply with applicable law.

## How long is information kept?

Correspondence is kept for the time necessary to answer the request and handle related follow-up. Retention depends on whether an issue remains open and whether a record is needed for applicable legal duties or claims. Information no longer needed for those purposes should be deleted; where retention is legally necessary, use and access are restricted accordingly. Provider security records and backups follow the providers’ retention rules.

## Cookies and website storage

This site does not set cookies or use browser local storage, advertising trackers, third-party analytics, embedded videos, or external fonts. Language is selected using the page controls or a URL parameter and is not stored in your browser. This description concerns this website; websites reached through external links have their own practices.

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction or erasure of your personal data, restriction of processing, and, where applicable, portability or objection to processing. To exercise these rights or ask about this notice, email [casaxmore@gmail.com](mailto:casaxmore@gmail.com). We may need proportionate information to verify your identity before handling a request.

You may lodge a complaint with the [Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD)](https://www.aepd.es/) or another competent supervisory authority.

## Security and updates

We use reasonable safeguards for support correspondence, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. This notice may be updated when the support service or its data handling changes; the date shown identifies this version.
