# Privacy Policy – Live Webcams: World Cameras

**Last updated: November 23, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Casaxmore, the developer of Live Webcams: World Cameras (“we”), handles information when you use the Live Webcams app for iOS and iPadOS (the “App”). If you have any questions, contact us at [casaxmore@gmail.com](mailto:casaxmore@gmail.com).

## Information handled by the App

Live Webcams does not require you to create an account. Casaxmore does not directly request or receive your name, email address, postal address, contacts, photos, payment details, searches, favorites, precise location, or camera images through its own servers.

### Public camera sources and network requests

The App lets you view still images and, where offered by a source, time-lapse videos from public cameras provided by official authorities and other authorized sources, including weather and environmental agencies, geological services, and national parks. Camera catalogs and media are requested directly from the relevant source or its media hosting service. Live Webcams does not record video or create a permanent library of camera images or videos.

Viewing public cameras does not require access to your device’s camera, microphone, or photo library. Camera images are held in memory for display; video playback may use temporary system buffers.

These connections necessarily expose ordinary technical request information, such as the device’s IP address, app or device information, and request details, to the relevant source operator. Each operator processes that information under its own terms and privacy practices. The applicable source, attribution, and license information are displayed in the App.

Matching searches against the camera catalog, applying category and favorite filters, and calculating distances are processed on your device. If a submitted place search has no matching cameras in the catalog, the App may send the search text to Apple Maps to locate that place. Casaxmore does not receive the content of your searches.

### Location

The optional Around Me feature requests access to your location while you use the App. Your location is used on the device to center the map and determine which public cameras are within the distance you select. The App does not request background location access, and Casaxmore does not receive or store your precise location.

You can deny or later revoke location permission in iOS or iPadOS Settings. Without location access, Around Me will not work, but you can continue exploring cameras through the map, search, category filters, and regional controls.

Live Webcams disables the Google Mobile Ads SDK’s automatic coarse-location collection setting before initializing the SDK and does not add the coordinates used by Around Me to ad requests. Google AdMob may still independently estimate a general location from the device’s IP address as described in the Advertising section. Disabling SDK location collection does not prevent IP-based estimation or other processing disclosed in that section.

### Favorites, local storage, and iCloud

The App stores favorite camera identifiers, a local indication of your Premium entitlement, expiration times for temporary access to individual continents, and a preference indicating whether you completed the introductory presentation.

The App also keeps a local cache of the public camera catalog to help display available cameras and refresh their information. This cache contains public camera details such as identifiers, names, coordinates, source links, and update information. It does not contain camera image files, your searches, or your device’s location. Camera images and videos are not included in the favorite or access records.

Temporary access expiration times are stored on your device and removed when the App checks expired access. They are not synchronized as favorites or sent to Casaxmore.

Favorite camera identifiers are stored locally and may also be synchronized through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage associated with your Apple account. Casaxmore does not operate the iCloud service and cannot directly access your iCloud account or synchronized favorites. Apple processes iCloud information under its own privacy terms.

You can remove individual favorites within the App. Deleting the App removes its locally stored data from the device, subject to backup and restoration features controlled by you and Apple. Favorites synchronized through iCloud may remain associated with your Apple account and may become available again if you reinstall the App. You can manage iCloud through your device and Apple account settings.

[Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

### Maps and sharing

The App uses Apple MapKit and Apple Maps to display maps and camera locations and, when needed, locate places entered in search. Apple may process the search text submitted to its service, the area of the map being requested, device and network information, and service interactions under its own privacy terms. Centering the map around your location also changes the map area requested from Apple. Casaxmore does not control Apple’s processing or retention.

Read [Apple Maps & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-maps/) for information about Apple’s map services.

If you use the App’s sharing feature, iOS or iPadOS presents the system share sheet. Camera information is sent only to the person, app, or service you select. Casaxmore does not receive the shared content. The selected recipient or service processes it under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Advertising

Unless Premium is active, Live Webcams uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. A rewarded ad is presented only after you select the option to watch one; eligible ads may be loaded in advance. Completing a rewarded ad temporarily unlocks the selected continent’s cameras for one hour. The App records the continent and its local expiration time; Casaxmore does not receive this access state through its own servers.

Live Webcams uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent where required. When Premium is not active, the App asks UMP for the current consent information on launch. The App does not initialize Google Mobile Ads or request an ad until UMP indicates that ads may be requested and, when Apple’s tracking decision has not previously been made, that decision has been completed. When required, you can review or change your UMP choices through Privacy options in the App’s settings.

After the UMP flow, the App may request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework. If you allow tracking, Google and its advertising partners may access the device’s advertising identifier and use app activity and device identifiers to deliver and measure personalized advertising across apps and websites. If you deny the request, the App does not access the advertising identifier for tracking. The App continues to work and may still display ads that comply with your consent choices and applicable restrictions. You can change the tracking permission later in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and applicable permissions, Google and its advertising partners may process your IP address for general location estimation, device or app identifiers that are available and permitted, advertising data, interactions with ads and the App, crash logs, performance data, and other diagnostics.

The App also supports install attribution through Apple’s SKAdNetwork, including when the advertising identifier is unavailable.

These data may be used to provide and measure ads, prevent fraud, perform analytics, maintain security, and improve advertising services. Ads may be personalized only where permitted by applicable law and your consent choices. Google and its partners determine their own processing and retention practices under their policies and may process information in other countries.

You can manage applicable tracking, privacy, and advertising controls through iOS or iPadOS Settings and Google’s services.

Casaxmore does not directly sell personal information.

## Purchases

Live Webcams offers an optional non-consumable Premium purchase that removes banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertising and permanently unlocks supported continents and the Premium features described in the App, including unlimited favorites.

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Casaxmore does not receive or store your full payment details. The App receives the transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock features and restore the purchase.

[Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

When you open an external website, the website and its hosting or service providers may receive information normally sent by a browser, such as your IP address and browser or device information. Their own privacy policies and terms apply.

Advertisements and external services may contain links to other websites or apps. This Privacy Policy does not govern third-party websites, apps, or services.

We use that information only to respond, provide support, protect our rights, and comply with applicable legal obligations. We retain correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

## Your choices and rights

You can:

- allow or revoke location permission in iOS or iPadOS Settings;
- use the map, search, filters, and regional controls without enabling Around Me;
- add or remove favorite cameras within the App;
- manage iCloud through your device and Apple account settings;
- review or change advertising consent choices through Privacy options when Google UMP makes that option available;
- allow or deny tracking permission and change that choice later in iOS or iPadOS Settings;
- choose whether to watch an optional rewarded ad for temporary access to a continent;
- purchase Premium and restore that purchase through the App Store;
- choose whether to open a camera source, license, or Casaxmore Apps website;
- choose whether and where to share camera information; and
- delete the App and its locally stored data, subject to Apple’s backup and restoration behavior.

Because searches, favorites, temporary access records, and precise location are not sent to Casaxmore, we cannot access, correct, export, or remotely delete that information for you. Favorites synchronized through your personal iCloud account are controlled through Apple’s services.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal information, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data protection authority. To make a request concerning information controlled by Casaxmore, contact [casaxmore@gmail.com](mailto:casaxmore@gmail.com). For information controlled independently by Apple, Google, a camera source operator, or another service you select, contact that provider directly.
